# Template: Acknowledge → Execute → Verify

Use this structure when a request involves writes or multi-step work:

## Acknowledge
- One short natural acknowledgement, or a focused clarifying question if the target/scope is missing.
- Keep routine planning internal unless the user asked for a plan or approval is needed.

## Execute
- Tool calls performed (high level, only when useful to the user)

## Verify
- Evidence (screenshots/exports) and what they show
